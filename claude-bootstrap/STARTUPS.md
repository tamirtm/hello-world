# Claude for Startups: status and application draft (checked 2026-10-18)

## Status: applications CLOSED right now
claude.com/programs/startups says the **current cohort is full** and they aren't accepting new applications.
Pending applications weren't moved forward. Subscribe to the Claude Startup newsletter on that page to hear when the next cohort opens.

## What it actually offers (Oct 6, 2026 terms, which differ from the video)
- **1 year of Claude Team with up to 5 Premium seats.** These are Team seats, not "5 Max subscriptions".
- **US$1,000 API credits (≈ C$1,390).** They're Console/first-party API only, not Bedrock or Vertex, and **expire 6 months** after the grant.
- Claude Startup Stack partner offers ("up to $45k" combined; not cash), Applied AI webinars, events.
- If you're VC-backed by an Anthropic partner fund: up to US$100k more credits through the VC.

## Eligibility (as reported for the Oct 2026 cohort; re-check the page when it reopens)
| Requirement | You? |
|---|---|
| Company founded in the last 5 years **or** raised funding in the last 2 years. Bootstrapped is OK | Likely yes if the business is ≤5 yrs old |
| You're an early-stage founder **building on Claude** | Must be true. Frame the product around your Claude automations |
| Claude Console account | Create one at platform.claude.com |
| **Company email on the same domain as your website** | ⚠️ A gmail.com address won't pass. Get a domain email (e.g. Google Workspace ≈ C$10/mo, or a free forwarder plus SMTP) |
| A live website on that domain | Needed to match the email |
No geographic restriction is listed, so Canada appears fine.

**Verdict:** you probably qualify **once** you have (1) a registered business, which in Manitoba can be a sole proprietorship via the Companies Office, (2) a website plus a matching email, and (3) a Claude-powered product to describe. Apply as soon as the next cohort opens, because decisions are often instant and the cohort fills fast.

## How to apply (when open)
1. Get the domain email working, and create the Console account with **that** email.
2. Go to claude.com/programs/startups and click Apply. Sign in to Console and fill in the form below.
3. Most decisions take minutes. Manual review takes 2–3 business days. When approved, redeem the credits and Stack codes in Console.
4. Put a Console workspace spend limit in place before you use the credits.
