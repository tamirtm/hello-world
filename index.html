How to play

Swipe or use the arrow keys (or WASD). The puck slides until it hits a wall or the edge. Ringed pads stop it mid-slide. Glide over every gem to clear the board — match par for three stars.

Keys: Z undo · R restart · H hint. The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone each day.
